# 🚀 Madadgar — Your AI-Powered Student Advisor

Madadgar is an intelligent platform built to help students navigate opportunities effortlessly.  
From overwhelming emails to missed deadlines, Madadgar transforms chaos into clarity through smart automation and personalized guidance.

---

## 💡 What is Madadgar?

Madadgar is more than just a tool — it’s a **personal AI advisor** designed specifically for students.

It understands your profile, analyzes incoming information, and helps you make smarter decisions about:
- Scholarships  
- Internships  
- Research programs  
- Career opportunities  

---

## ⚡ How It Works

Madadgar combines multiple intelligent systems into one seamless experience:

### 🧠 Intelligent Email Analysis
Madadgar scans and understands email content — especially `.edu` sources — and identifies whether an email contains a valuable opportunity.

It extracts key details such as:
- Deadlines  
- Benefits  
- Type of opportunity  
- Required actions  

---

### 🎯 Smart Opportunity Ranking
Not all opportunities are equally important — and Madadgar knows that.

Each opportunity is ranked based on:
- Urgency  
- Importance  
- Relevance to the student  

This ensures you always see **what matters most first**.

---

### 👤 Personalized Student Profiling
Madadgar builds a detailed understanding of you through your profile:
- Academic background  
- Skills and interests  
- Career goals  
- Preferences  

This allows the system to **tailor recommendations specifically to you**, not just generic results.

---

### 🤖 Madadgar Advisor (AI Chatbot)
At the core of the platform is the **Madadgar Advisor** — your personal AI assistant.

It can:
- Suggest what to apply for  
- Help write professional emails  
- Provide career guidance  
- Answer student-related queries  

All responses are **profile-aware and context-driven**, making them highly relevant.

---

### 📊 Unified Dashboard Experience
Madadgar brings everything together in one place:
- Ranked opportunities  
- Insights and suggestions  
- Easy-to-understand results  

No clutter. No confusion. Just clarity.

---
