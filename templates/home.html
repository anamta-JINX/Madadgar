FAQ

Quick questions.

Do I need to connect my email account? +
No. You can simply paste up to 20 emails as text. We're working on optional Gmail and Outlook integrations for next term — until then, copy-paste keeps things private and zero-setup.
How does the ranking actually work? +
Each email is scored against your student profile — current courses, role, key deadlines, and history of what you've marked important. Urgency tiers (Act today / This week / Can wait) are based on date sensitivity, sender authority, and required action.
Is my email data private? +
Yes. Emails are processed in-session and not stored. We never train on student content. Your profile stays on your device unless you opt into sync.
What are the chatbot's guardrails? +
The chatbot only answers questions grounded in your emails and student context. It declines off-topic asks, won't give legal/medical/financial advice, and flags anything that looks like phishing or coercion rather than helping you respond to it.
Is it really free? +
Free for individual students with a verified .edu address during beta. We'll introduce paid plans for institutions and heavy users later — your beta access stays free.
